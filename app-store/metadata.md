---

## ⚠️ Claims to remove / avoid before submission

The following types of claims **will cause App Store rejection or guideline violation:**

| Type | Example to avoid | Why |
|------|-----------------|-----|
| Superlative | "most accurate", "best tarot" | Unverifiable — Guideline 2.3.1 |
| Future prediction | "see what tomorrow holds" | Implies factual prediction |
| Medical/mental health | "reduces anxiety", "healing" | Health claims require evidence |
| "Free" in name/subtitle | — | App has IAP — Guideline 3.1.1 |
| "Professional readings" | — | Implies licensed service |

**Safe framing:** "reflects on", "explore", "daily reflection", "a moment for yourself", "draw inspiration from"

## Notes before submission

1. **IAP must be configured** in App Store Connect before submitting — Apple reviewers will test the paywall flow.
2. **Account deletion** must be accessible in-app (you have it in Settings ✓).
3. **Review notes** to add: *"This app contains a subscription paywall. To test without purchasing: use the [test account if provided] or simply view the free tier. The app does not require login to access the daily card and general horoscope."*
4. **Encryption**: if using HTTPS only (Supabase), answer "Yes, uses standard HTTPS encryption — exempt from export compliance."
