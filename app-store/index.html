Arcana: Night Sky & Moon

Tonight's Moon, planets, satellite passes and sky events for your exact location — computed on your device, accurate even offline. And one quiet minute a day to write it down.

🌒 Coming soon to the App Store · Free, with optional Premium

Your sky, tonight

Real astronomy for the place you actually stand — no account, no waiting for a server.

🕗Best time to observe

A nightly window computed from darkness, moonlight and what's above the horizon — with a short tour of what to look at first.

🌗The Moon, in detail

Phase, illumination, rise and set with compass bearings, distance and apparent size, perigee and apogee — plus a full month calendar of Moon phases.

🪐Planets you can actually see

Visibility windows with rise, highest point and set times, altitude, azimuth and magnitude — observational data, not horoscopes.

🛰️ISS over your head

Naked-eye pass predictions for your location, with direction and brightness, so you know exactly when to look up.

☄️Sky events, with reminders

Eclipses, meteor-shower peaks, solstices and equinoxes in your local time — tap the bell and the app reminds you the evening it matters.

📓A minute of reflection

One grounded question from today's real sky, your mood, a few lines. Saved on your device; sign-in is optional.

No predictions — that's the point. Arcana shows what the sky is doing and asks reflective questions. It never predicts your future. Classic card readings and horoscopes exist as optional extras, tucked away in the Menu — the app leads with the real night sky.

Arcana Premium

Optional subscription. Everything above stays free.

  • Unlimited saved observing places
  • Reflection insights: a 30-day mood mix and streaks
  • Satellite pack: Tiangong and Hubble passes
  • Full history of your entries and readings

Private by design

All astronomy is computed locally with a scientific ephemeris library. Works fully offline. No ads, no tracking of your location — the observing place you pick never leaves your device unless you sign in to sync your journal.
