## Description (≤4000)

```
Arcana turns a quick daily check-in into a calm, grounded ritual — a tarot card, your horoscope, and zodiac compatibility, all in one quiet place.

A DAILY RITUAL
• Card of the Day — draw one card and read a clear, grounded interpretation.
• Daily Horoscope — energy, love, and career themes for your sign.
• Compatibility — a two-chart reading for any pair, across Sun, Moon, Venus and Mars.
• Tarot Oracle — choose a theme, ask your question, and receive a woven, multi-card reading.
• Card Library — all 78 tarot cards with upright and reversed meanings.
• Streaks & progress — keep your daily practice going.

GROUNDED, NOT FORTUNE-TELLING
Arcana speaks in themes, patterns, and reflection — never guarantees or predictions about the future. It is a tool for self-reflection and entertainment.

ARCANA PREMIUM
Unlock the full practice:
• Unlimited tarot sessions, plus 3-card and 5-card spreads
• Daily Love and Career horoscope themes
• Full compatibility reports with a complete score breakdown
• Saved reading history to revisit your patterns over time

Start with today's card. Build a daily rhythm that helps you slow down, reflect, and notice what matters.

Arcana Premium is an auto-renewable subscription.
• Options: Monthly or Yearly. Price is shown in the app before purchase.
• Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.

• Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.
```
