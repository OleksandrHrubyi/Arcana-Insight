Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 28 June 2026

Arcana Insight is a self-reflection app. We collect only what is strictly necessary to operate the service. We do not sell your data to anyone.

1. Who We Are

Arcana Insight ("we", "our", "the app") is an independent mobile application available on the Apple App Store. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at ghrubyi@ukr.net.

2. What Data We Collect

Data Why we collect it Required?

Date of birth Generating your personalised horoscope based on your zodiac sign No (Premium feature)

We do not collect: location data, contacts, photos, browsing history, health data, or any biometric information.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalise app features (daily card, horoscope, rituals)
  • To process and verify your subscription status
  • To deliver optional push notifications you explicitly enable
  • To measure feature usage, purchase funnel performance, and app reliability
  • To respond to support requests
  • To maintain service reliability and fix bugs

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to data brokers, or use it for cross-app tracking.

We do not use advertising SDKs or third-party tracking pixels. Analytics is used only for operating and improving Arcana Insight.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS, EU region). All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We use row-level security policies so that each user can only access their own data.

Daily ritual notes and intentions are stored locally on your device using encrypted device storage (Capacitor Preferences). They are not uploaded to our servers unless you are signed in and syncing is enabled.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (see below), personal data associated with your account is deleted or scheduled for deletion from our systems within 30 days.

Notification tokens and notification preferences are kept while notifications remain enabled or until they become inactive and are rotated out.

Aggregate analytics and technical event data may be retained for longer where needed to monitor app performance, prevent abuse, and investigate product issues.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (e.g. wrong date of birth)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your saved readings and ritual data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect prior processing)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ghrubyi@ukr.net.

8. How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly inside the app:

  • Open the app → Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears

This permanently deletes your email address, birth date, saved readings, ritual history, and all other personal data from our servers. Deletion is irreversible.

If you are unable to access the app, you can also request deletion by emailing ghrubyi@ukr.net from the email address associated with your account.

9. Children's Privacy

Arcana Insight is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for all ages. However, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union) without verifiable parental consent.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at ghrubyi@ukr.net and we will delete it promptly.

10. International Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR. Our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Contract performance — to provide the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interest — to maintain app security and prevent fraud
  • Consent — for optional features like personal horoscope (you can withdraw at any time)

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when we add new features or change how we process data. If changes are material, we will notify you via an in-app notice. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
