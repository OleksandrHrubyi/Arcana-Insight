Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025  ·  Last updated: 1 January 2025

Arcana Insight is a self-reflection app. We collect only what is strictly necessary to operate the service. We do not sell your data to anyone.

1. Who We Are

Arcana Insight ("we", "our", "the app") is an independent mobile application available on the Apple App Store. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at support@arcana-insight.app.

2. What Data We Collect

Data Why we collect it Required?
Email address Account creation and login (optional — you can use the app without an account) No
Date of birth Generating your personalised horoscope based on your zodiac sign No (Premium feature)
Anonymous device identifier Assigning a consistent daily tarot card and horoscope without requiring login Yes (automatically generated)
Subscription status Determining whether you have an active Premium subscription Only if you subscribe
Daily ritual data (morning intention, evening mood) Displaying your entries inside the app; building your daily streak No (optional feature)
Saved tarot readings Letting you revisit past readings inside the app No (optional feature)

We do not collect: location data, contacts, photos, browsing history, health data, or any biometric information.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalise app features (daily card, horoscope, rituals)
  • To process and verify your subscription status
  • To respond to support requests
  • To maintain service reliability and fix bugs

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the app.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to run the app. Each has its own privacy policy.

Service Purpose Data shared
Supabase Database and authentication (stores your account data, horoscopes, readings) Email, device ID, ritual data, readings
RevenueCat Subscription management and validation Anonymous user ID, subscription status
OpenAI Generating horoscope text — called server-side only; no user-identifiable data is sent to OpenAI Date, zodiac sign, astronomical context only
Apple App Store Payment processing for subscriptions Handled entirely by Apple

We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or tracking pixels.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS, EU region). All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We use row-level security policies so that each user can only access their own data.

Daily ritual notes and intentions are stored locally on your device using encrypted device storage (Capacitor Preferences). They are not uploaded to our servers unless you are signed in and syncing is enabled.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (see below), all personal data associated with your account is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Anonymous usage data (device ID, saved readings without an account) is retained for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data (e.g. wrong date of birth)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your saved readings and ritual data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect prior processing)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@arcana-insight.app.

8. How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly inside the app:

  • Open the app → Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears

This permanently deletes your email address, birth date, saved readings, ritual history, and all other personal data from our servers. Deletion is irreversible.

If you are unable to access the app, you can also request deletion by emailing support@arcana-insight.app from the email address associated with your account.

9. Children's Privacy

Arcana Insight is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for all ages. However, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union) without verifiable parental consent.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@arcana-insight.app and we will delete it promptly.

10. International Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR. Our legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Contract performance — to provide the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interest — to maintain app security and prevent fraud
  • Consent — for optional features like personal horoscope (you can withdraw at any time)

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when we add new features or change how we process data. If changes are material, we will notify you via an in-app notice. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

12. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your privacy:

Email: support@arcana-insight.app

We aim to respond within 5 business days.
