Subscriptions & Billing

Subscription

What does Arcana Insight Premium include?

Premium unlocks the following features:

  • Personal horoscope based on your birth date
  • Full tarot sessions with deeper readings and more spread formats
  • Extra horoscope themes, including love and career
  • Compatibility readings
  • Saving readings to revisit later

The daily card and general daily horoscope are always free.

Billing

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. To cancel:

  • Open Settings on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top → Subscriptions
  • Find Arcana Insight and tap Cancel Subscription

You will retain Premium access until the end of your current billing period. No refund is issued for the remaining period.

Billing

I was charged but Premium isn't active in the app. What do I do?

This is usually a sync issue. Try the following:

  • Open the app → Premium → Restore Purchases
  • Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase
  • Force-close and reopen the app

If the issue persists, email us at ghrubyi@ukr.net and include your Apple ID email and approximate purchase date.

Billing

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. To request a refund:

  • Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Sign in with your Apple ID and find the charge
  • Select "I'd like to request a refund"

Apple's standard policy allows refunds within 14 days of purchase.

Account

Account

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. You can use the daily card and general horoscope without creating an account. An account is only needed if you want to sync data across devices or use features tied to your profile (like the personal horoscope).

Account

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  • Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • Confirm in the dialog that appears

This permanently removes your email address, birth date, saved readings, ritual history, and all personal data from our servers. The action cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email ghrubyi@ukr.net from the address linked to your account and we will delete it manually.

Account

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

Content & Features

Content

Why is my daily card the same as yesterday?

Each day you receive a unique card generated by a deterministic daily seed — the same card stays with you throughout the entire day. At midnight (your local time) the card resets and a new one is drawn. If the day has already changed and you're still seeing the same card, try closing and reopening the app.

Content

Is the horoscope the same for everyone with my sign?

The general daily horoscope is the same for all users sharing the same sun sign. The personal horoscope (Premium feature) takes your birth date into account and generates a more specific reading shaped by your sign's current energy.

Content

The horoscope isn't loading. What should I do?

Horoscopes require an internet connection — they are generated fresh each day. Please check your connection and try again. If the problem persists for more than a day, contact us so we can check whether generation failed on our end.

Technical Issues

Technical

The app crashes or freezes. How do I fix it?

  • Force-close the app and reopen it
  • Make sure you have the latest version installed (App Store → Updates)
  • Restart your iPhone
  • If the issue continues, uninstall and reinstall the app (your account data will be restored after login)

If none of the above helps, email us with your device model and iOS version.

Technical

My streak reset even though I used the app. What happened?

Streaks are calculated based on whether you opened the daily card, read the horoscope, or completed a tarot session on each calendar day. If you used the app after midnight but before checking these features on the new day, the previous day may have been counted as missed. Please email us if you believe this was an error.
