Subscriptions & Billing

What does Arcana Premium include?

Premium is an optional auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly). It unlocks:

  • Unlimited saved observing places
  • Reflection insights: a 30-day mood mix and streaks
  • Satellite pack: naked-eye passes of Tiangong and Hubble
  • Full history of your journal entries and readings
  • Unlimited readings, plus 3-card and 5-card spreads

Everything else — tonight's sky, the Moon calendar, planets, ISS passes, sky events with reminders and the daily reflection — is free and stays free.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top → Subscriptions
  3. Find Arcana and tap Cancel Subscription

You keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.

I was charged but Premium isn't active. What do I do?

This is usually a sync issue:

  1. Open the app → Premium → Restore Purchases
  2. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase
  3. Force-close and reopen the app

If it persists, email ghrubyi@ukr.net with your Apple ID email and the approximate purchase date.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple:

  1. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID and find the charge
  3. Select "I'd like to request a refund"

Apple's standard policy allows refunds within 14 days of purchase.

App & Features

Do the sky calculations need the internet?

No. The Moon, planets, rise/set times, the calendar and sky events are computed on your device with a scientific ephemeris library — they work fully offline and stay accurate. Only optional extras (weather overlay for observing conditions, journal sync, readings) use the network.

Times look shifted after I changed my observing place — why?

All times on the sky screens follow the time zone of your observing place, not your device. If you're in one country and your saved place is in another, the app shows the sky for that place — and adds a UTC offset label so the difference is explicit. That's intended: the sky you're planning for is the one above your chosen location.

How do sky-event reminders work?

Tap the bell next to any event (meteor peak, eclipse, solstice). The app schedules a local notification for the evening of the event in your local time. You can turn each reminder off the same way — no account needed.

Account & Data

Do I need an account?

No. The whole sky toolset and the daily reflection work without an account. Signing in is only needed to sync your journal and saved data across devices.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  2. Confirm in the dialog

This permanently removes your email, saved entries, history and all personal data from our servers. The action cannot be undone. If you can't access the app, email ghrubyi@ukr.net from the address linked to your account and we'll delete it manually.

I forgot my password.

On the login screen tap Forgot password? and enter your email. A reset link arrives within a few minutes — check spam if it doesn't.
