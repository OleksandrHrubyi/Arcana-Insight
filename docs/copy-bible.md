# Copy Bible

This file defines how Arcana Insight should sound across tarot, horoscope, onboarding, premium, and UI microcopy.

## Brand Voice

Arcana Insight should sound:

- calm
- grounded
- premium
- concise
- emotionally intelligent

It should not sound:

- sensational
- manipulative
- vague for the sake of sounding mystical
- pushy
- AI-generated

## Core Principles

1. Utility first.
   The user should understand what the screen or reading helps them do.

2. Reflection, not prophecy theater.
   Speak in themes, insight, direction, and reflection.

3. Short mobile language.
   Most screens should not depend on long explanatory copy.

4. Trust over hype.
   Especially on premium, onboarding, and locked states.

## Approved Tone Patterns

Prefer language like:

- `Today's theme is focus and clarity.`
- `Take one grounded step today.`
- `This reading points to patience, not urgency.`
- `Premium adds more context and deeper interpretation.`
- `Complete today's ritual to keep your streak.`

## Avoid

- `The universe demands...`
- `Act now before this energy disappears.`
- `Your destiny is blocked.`
- `This guarantees love, money, or success.`
- `You must unlock this or miss your chance.`

## Tarot Copy Rules

- Base tarot copy on real card meanings already present in the app.
- Use teaser language on compact surfaces and full interpretation on dedicated reading surfaces.
- Speak in themes, patterns, choices, and grounded next steps.
- Do not overstate certainty.

Good:

- `The card points to a quieter kind of progress.`
- `Notice where structure helps and where it turns rigid.`

Bad:

- `This card proves your relationship will fail.`
- `A major breakthrough is guaranteed today.`

## Horoscope Copy Rules

- Keep horoscope summaries readable in one glance.
- Theme labels should be concrete and understandable.
- Deeper horoscope text may be reflective, but still should lead to a practical takeaway.

Good:

- `In career, simplify your priorities and protect your energy.`
- `In love, clarity matters more than speed today.`

Bad:

- `Your stars are exploding with destiny.`
- `Everything will change instantly if you follow this sign.`

## Premium Copy Rules

- Explain product value, not cosmic status.
- Use concrete unlocks:
  - unlimited readings
  - more themes
  - deeper interpretation
  - compatibility
  - history
- Keep billing language calm and direct.

Good:

- `Unlock unlimited tarot sessions and deeper interpretation.`
- `See love and career horoscope themes with Premium.`

Bad:

- `Upgrade to reveal your true fate.`
- `Without Premium, your path stays hidden.`

## Onboarding Copy Rules

- Explain the product value fast.
- Avoid aesthetic filler before function.
- Keep steps short and obvious.

Good:

- `Get a daily card, practical horoscope, and tarot guidance in one place.`
- `Choose what matters most so your first session feels relevant.`

Bad:

- `Enter the portal of infinite intuitive expansion.`

## Lock State Rules

- Explain what is locked and why it matters.
- Never shame the free user.
- Never imply the free experience is fake.

Good:

- `Premium unlocks deeper spreads and reading history.`

Bad:

- `You are blocked until you upgrade.`

## Safety Rules

Do not imply deterministic outcomes about:

- health
- fertility
- legal matters
- financial guarantees
- emergencies
- relationship certainty

## UI Microcopy Rules

- Buttons should use direct verbs.
- CTA labels should describe the action.
- Helper text should be short and readable.
- Empty states should tell the user what to do next.

Prefer:

- `Open today's card`
- `Continue reading`
- `Unlock Premium`
- `Restore purchases`

Avoid:

- `Tap here`
- `Proceed`
- `Magic starts now`

## Translation Rule

English and Ukrainian should preserve the same product meaning and emotional weight, not drift into different concepts.
